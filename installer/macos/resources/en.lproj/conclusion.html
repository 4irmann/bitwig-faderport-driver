Installation complete

Next steps:

  1. Connect the FaderPort (the additional power supply is recommended for the motor fader).
  2. Start Bitwig Studio and open Settings > Controllers.
  3. If the FaderPort isn't detected automatically, click Add controller > PreSonus > FaderPort Classic (4irmann) and select the FaderPort as MIDI input and output.

Don't use the entries "FaderPort 8/16"; they are for the newer models.

Manual: github.com/4irmann/bitwig-faderport-driver

To uninstall, delete ~/Documents/Bitwig Studio/Extensions/FaderPort4irmann.bwextension.
